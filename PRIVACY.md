# Pleats privacy policy

Pleats does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
- The accordion sound is synthesized on your device. Nothing you play leaves the device.
- Recordings you make are saved in the app's Documents folder on your device (visible in the Files app). They are only shared if you share them yourself.
- Song progress and settings are stored on your device.
- Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; Pleats only receives whether Pleats Pro is unlocked.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/heyobi/pleats/issues
